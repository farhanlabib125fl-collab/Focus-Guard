# 🛡️ Focus Guard

**Focus Guard** is a free, lightweight, and open-source desktop application designed to eliminate digital distractions and boost your daily productivity. Inspired by tools like Cold Turkey, it helps you stay focused on your work or study sessions by locking away distracting applications and websites.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🚫 **Distraction Blocker:** Effortlessly block distracting apps and software on your PC.
- ⚡ **Minimal & Lightweight:** Built using **Electron.js** and styled with **Tailwind CSS** for a clean, fast UI.
- 🔒 **Privacy-First:** All your settings and blocked lists stay stored locally on your device.
- 🆓 **100% Free & Open-Source:** No hidden subscriptions, ads, or locked premium features.

---

## 📥 Download & Installation

Getting started with Focus Guard is super simple!

1. Head over to the official **[Releases Page](https://github.com/farhanlabib125fl-collab/Focus-Guard/releases)**.
2. Download the latest installer (`.exe` file) for Windows.
3. Run the installer and launch **Focus Guard**!
